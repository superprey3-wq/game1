# Third-party art used by the Unity rebuild

## Kenney Top-down Shooter
- Source mirror: `ETdoFresh/kenney.nl/topdown-shooter`
- Original source: Kenney
- License: CC0 1.0
- Current planned uses: Captain Rain base sprite, robot enemies, infected runner placeholders, weapons and environment tiles.

## Kenney Alien UFO Pack
- Source mirror: `ETdoFresh/kenney.nl/alien-ufo-pack`
- Original source: Kenney
- License: CC0 1.0
- Current planned uses: laser projectiles, impact bursts and sci-fi props.

The Unity editor menu `Arena Satellites > Art > Download CC0 Kenney Starter Set` downloads a curated starter subset into `Assets/Art/Kenney` and configures the PNG files as Unity sprites.

These packs are deliberately used only as the foundation of the visual rebuild. Hero portraits, major bosses, biome hero pieces, lighting, particles and composition will be authored/curated separately so the final game has one coherent high-detail sci-fi look rather than the appearance of an unmodified asset pack.
